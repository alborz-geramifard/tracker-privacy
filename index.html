Privacy Policy — Tracker

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Tracker is a personal habit tracking app. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

Data We Collect

Tracker does not collect any personal data. All habit data (names and completion dates) is stored locally on your device.

iCloud Sync (Pro)

If you enable iCloud Sync, your habit data is stored in your personal iCloud account via Apple's Key-Value Storage. We never see or have access to it. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not collect or store payment information.

Analytics & Tracking

Tracker contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No data leaves your device.

Data Deletion

Delete the app to remove all local data. To remove iCloud data, manage your iCloud storage in Settings.

Contact

Questions? Email: support@trackerapp.app
